Photo Release & Media Usage

A photo booth takes pictures of people. This page sets out plainly who can use those pictures, how, and how to say no.

1. What This Covers

This policy applies to every photo, GIF, boomerang, and video our booth captures at an event, and to how those images are delivered, shared, and used afterwards. It forms part of our Terms of Service and works alongside our Privacy Policy.

2. Guests Using The Booth

The booth is voluntary. Nobody has to use it.

By stepping into the booth and taking a photo, a guest understands that the image is captured, shown on screen, printed if prints are part of the package, and made available in the event gallery that the client shares.

The booth also offers filters, emoji overlays, and digital props. A guest chooses whether to apply these, and they form part of the final image that is printed and delivered.

We recommend that clients let guests know a photo booth will be at the event, and we are happy to provide a short notice for you to display next to the booth. See section 8.

3. What The Client Can Do With The Photos

You, the client, may use your event photos freely. Print them, frame them, post them, put them in a company newsletter, or send them to every guest. There is no additional licence fee and no restriction on personal or internal business use.

For corporate clients, this includes using the images in your own marketing, subject to you having the consents you need from the people shown.

4. What We Use Photos For

We use event photos to run and deliver the service: showing them on the booth screen, printing them, and publishing your gallery.

We may also use a selection of images to promote our business, for example on our website, on Instagram, in a portfolio shown to prospective clients, or in advertising. This is how a photo booth business shows what it does.

When we do this, we will not:

  • Sell your photos to a third party as stock imagery.
  • Use photos in a way that is defamatory, or that suggests a person endorses a product or political position.
  • Publish a photo that a client, or a guest shown in it, has asked us not to publish.

5. How To Opt Out

Clients. Tell us you do not want any photos from your event used for our marketing. The cleanest time is before the event, ideally in writing when you sign your agreement, but you can ask at any point and we will stop using them going forward and remove what we have already posted on channels we control.

Guests. If you appear in a photo we have published and you want it taken down, email us and tell us which image. We will remove it from channels we control. We cannot force removal of a copy that somebody else has already downloaded and reposted.

Opt-out requests go to flashinglightsphotobooth11@gmail.com.

6. Children

Where a child appears in event photos, the booking client or the child’s parent or guardian decides how those photos may be used.

We will not use an image in which a child is identifiable for our marketing if the client, or a parent or guardian, asks us not to. If you would like a blanket rule for your event, tell us in advance and we will apply it.

7. Sharing, Hashtags and Social Media

Where your package includes social sharing or hashtag printing, guests can send their own photos to themselves and post them. Once a guest posts an image to their own account, that platform’s terms apply and the image is outside our control and yours.

If your event uses a custom hashtag, be aware that it makes images easier for others to find and reshare.

8. Corporate Events and Brand Activations

For branded activations, additional terms usually apply and your agreement with us will set them out. Typically:

  • You supply logos, brand assets, and overlay artwork, and you confirm you have the right to use them.
  • You keep ownership of your brand assets. We use them only for your activation.
  • Where the activation captures guest details, such as an email for sending photos, the responsibility for notices and consents for that data capture is set out in your agreement.
  • You may ask us to keep the activation confidential and out of our portfolio, and we will.

We can provide a short printed notice for display at the booth, telling guests that photos are being taken, who is taking them, and how they will be used. We recommend using one at every event, and for corporate activations we recommend your legal team approves the wording.

9. Ownership

We own the copyright in the images our booth captures, as the creator of the work. You receive a broad, perpetual, royalty-free licence to use the images from your event for any personal or internal business purpose, as described in section 3.

Custom templates and overlays we design remain our creative work, except for your own brand assets, which remain yours.

10. How Long We Keep Images

Galleries stay available for a limited period after your event. Download your photos before the gallery expires. We may keep selected images in our portfolio for longer, subject to the opt-out rights in section 5. If you want your event media deleted from our systems, tell us and we will delete the copies we hold.

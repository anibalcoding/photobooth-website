1. Who We Are

Flashing Lights Photo Booth ("Flashing Lights," "we," "us," or "our") is a photo booth rental business based in Dallas, Texas, serving the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex and traveling statewide and nationally for events.

This policy covers our website, our booking and enquiry process, and the photo booth services we provide at events.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us directly. When you submit our booking enquiry form, we ask for your first name, last name, email address, phone number, event type, event date, and any details you choose to share about your event, such as venue, guest count, and branding or theme preferences.

Booking and contract information. If you move forward with a booking, we collect the information needed to prepare and perform your service agreement. This includes your event location, event start and end times, package selection, pricing, and your signature on the agreement. We use an electronic signature service to send and store signed agreements.

Payment information. We record the amounts you have paid and what remains outstanding. If you pay by card or through a payment app, that payment is processed by a third party. We do not collect or store full card numbers or bank login details on our systems.

Event media. At your event, our booth captures photos, and depending on your package, GIFs, boomerangs, and video. These images contain the likenesses of you and your guests, and may include anything visible in frame, such as clothing, props, backdrops, and branding. Guests may also apply filters, emoji overlays, and digital props to their photos. Section 5 explains how that works.

Communications. If you email, call, text, or message us on social media, we keep those messages so we can answer you and keep a record of what was agreed.

Information collected automatically. Our website does not use cookies, advertising pixels, or third-party analytics. Our web host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, for security and reliability. If we add analytics or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy first.

3. How We Use Information

  • To respond to your enquiry and give you a quote.
  • To prepare, send, and keep your service agreement.
  • To plan and deliver your event, including designing custom print templates.
  • To process payments and keep accurate financial records.
  • To deliver your photos, prints, and online gallery.
  • To contact you about your booking, including confirmations and reminders.
  • To promote our business, subject to the limits in section 5 and in our Photo Release and Media Usage Policy.
  • To meet our legal, tax, and accounting obligations, and to resolve disputes.

4. Why We Are Allowed To Use It

We process your information because it is necessary to provide the service you asked us for, because we have a legitimate business interest in running and promoting our business, because we need to comply with the law, or because you have given us permission. Where we rely on your permission, you can withdraw it at any time by contacting us.

5. Photos and Your Likeness

Photo booth photos are, by their nature, pictures of people. We treat them carefully.

We use event photos to deliver your gallery and prints. We may also use selected photos to promote our business, for example on our website, Instagram, or in a portfolio shown to prospective clients.

  • You can ask us not to use any photos from your event for marketing. The cleanest time to tell us is before your event, but you can ask at any time.
  • A guest who appears in a photo we have published can ask us to take it down, and we will remove it from channels we control.
  • For corporate and brand activations, marketing use is governed by your agreement with us, which may restrict it further.

Our full approach to photos is set out in our Photo Release and Media Usage Policy.

Our booth offers filters, emoji overlays, and digital props. To place these on screen, the booth software detects where facial features are in the frame, in real time, so the graphic lands in the right place. This is face detection used only to position a graphic. It does not identify anyone, it is not compared against any database, and no record of facial geometry is kept once the photo is taken.

We do not use facial recognition. If we ever introduce a feature that captures or stores a biometric identifier as that term is defined by Texas law, we will give clear notice and obtain consent before it is used at your event.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for targeted advertising. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers. Companies that help us operate, such as our photo booth software provider, online gallery host, electronic signature provider, email provider, payment processor, and printing suppliers. They may only use the information to perform services for us.
  • Your event contacts. If you ask us to coordinate with your planner, venue, or a colleague, we will share what is needed to run the event.
  • Guests at your event. By design, guests receive their own photos at the booth and through the gallery link you choose to distribute.
  • Legal and safety. Where we must comply with the law, enforce our agreement, or protect the rights and safety of people or property.
  • Business transfer. If the business is sold or reorganised, information may transfer as part of that transaction.

7. How Long We Keep Information

  • Enquiries that do not become bookings: kept for a reasonable period so we can follow up, then deleted.
  • Booking records, signed agreements, and payment records: kept for as long as needed for tax, accounting, and legal purposes.
  • Event photos and galleries: kept for a limited period after your event so you and your guests can download them. Ask us for your gallery expiry date, and download your photos before then. If you want your event media deleted sooner, tell us and we will delete the copies we hold.
  • Photos selected for our portfolio: may be kept for longer, subject to your right to object under section 5.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we hold, including limiting who can access it and relying on reputable providers for storage and payments. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we will tell you promptly if a breach affects your information in a way that requires notification.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Texas residents have rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, and residents of other states may have similar rights. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong.
  • Ask us to delete your personal information.
  • Object to our use of your photos for marketing.
  • Opt out of marketing messages at any time.
  • Appeal if we refuse a request.

To make a request, email flashinglightsphotobooth11@gmail.com. We may need to verify who you are before we act, and we will respond within the time the law allows. There are limits: for example, we may need to keep records of a completed booking for tax purposes even if you ask us to delete them.

10. Children

Our website and booking process are intended for adults, and we do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Children do attend the events we work at and do use the booth. Where a child appears in event photos, we treat the parent, guardian, or booking client as the person who decides how those photos may be used, and we will not use photos in which a child is identifiable for marketing if the client or a parent or guardian asks us not to.

11. Other Websites

Our site links to third-party sites such as Instagram and Google. Once you follow a link, that company’s own privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for how they handle your information.

12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as our business changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change to how we use your information, we will take reasonable steps to tell you.

Cancellation & Refund Policy

We are a service business, not a shop, so there is nothing to return. What matters instead is what happens to your money if a date changes or an event is called off. That is what this page covers.

1. The Short Version

  • The retainer that reserves your date is non-refundable.
  • The remaining balance is due two (2) days before your event.
  • If you cancel, any balance you have already paid beyond the retainer is refunded.
  • Rescheduling is possible if we have your new date open, and the retainer moves with you.
  • If we cannot perform, you get a full refund, including the retainer.

The sections below explain each of these.

2. Why The Retainer Is Non-Refundable

When you book, we take your date off the market. We stop quoting it to other couples and companies, and we turn away enquiries for it. That lost opportunity is not recoverable, which is why the retainer is non-refundable once the date is reserved.

The retainer is credited toward your total price. It is not an extra fee.

3. If You Cancel

  • Cancel at any time and the retainer is not refunded.
  • Any amount you have paid above the retainer is refunded in full.
  • If your balance is unpaid at the point you cancel, nothing further is owed, unless your agreement provides otherwise.
  • Cancellations should be sent in writing, by email, so there is a clear record and date.

4. Rescheduling

We would always rather move your date than lose your booking.

  • Rescheduling is subject to our availability on the new date. Please ask as early as you can, because popular dates go quickly.
  • If we are available, your retainer transfers to the new date and nothing extra is charged for the change itself.
  • If the new date falls in a higher-demand period, or your requirements change, pricing may be adjusted. We will tell you before you commit.
  • If we are not available on your new date, the booking is treated as a cancellation by you and section 3 applies.
  • We will normally accommodate one reschedule per booking. Further changes are at our discretion.

5. Unpaid Balance and No-Shows

The balance is due two days before your event. If it has not been paid and we have not agreed something different in writing, we may treat the booking as cancelled by you and we are not obliged to attend.

If we arrive at your venue and cannot set up because nobody is there to give us access, because there is no suitable space, or because there is no power available, and this is not resolved within a reasonable time, the booking is treated as performed and no refund is due.

6. If We Cannot Perform

If we cancel your booking for any reason within our control, you receive a full refund of everything you have paid, including the retainer.

We will also do our best to help you find a replacement provider, though we cannot guarantee one will be available.

7. Weather, Venue Problems and Other Events Outside Control

If your event cannot go ahead because of something neither of us controls, such as severe weather, natural disaster, venue closure, power failure at the venue, government restriction, road closure, accident, or serious illness, we will first try to reschedule you to an open date, with the retainer transferring.

If no reschedule is possible, we will refund amounts you have paid beyond the retainer. The retainer remains non-refundable, because the date was still held for you and still turned others away.

8. Equipment Failure and Service Interruptions

We maintain our equipment and bring backups where practical. If a technical fault on our side means the booth is unavailable for a meaningful part of your contracted hours, and we cannot make up that time at your event, we will refund a fair, proportionate amount of your package price for the time lost.

No refund is due for time lost because of venue conditions, power supplied by the venue, guest behaviour, or how your event schedule runs. Section 9 of our Terms of Service sets this out in more detail.

9. Problems With Your Photos

If there is a problem with your prints or your digital delivery, tell us as soon as you notice it and we will put it right where we can, for example by re-delivering files, re-issuing a gallery link, or reprinting.

Please raise any issue with your gallery promptly, and always download your photos before your gallery expires.

10. How To Cancel, Reschedule, or Request a Refund

Email flashinglightsphotobooth11@gmail.com with your name, event date, and what you would like to do. Please put your request in writing so there is a clear record of the date you made it.

We aim to respond to every message within 24 hours. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method where possible, normally within 10 business days of agreeing the refund.

11. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us first. Raising a chargeback without contacting us does not change what this policy or your signed agreement says, and we will respond to the payment provider with your agreement and this policy.
